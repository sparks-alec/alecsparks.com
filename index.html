Alec Sparks, Technical Director

I make custom hardware and software that help people make art and put on live events.

I sell custom gear at Stage Blocks Co, keep directories of resources for nerds who put on shows at Stage Hacks and OSC.fyi, and help run The Voxel, a theatrical R&D center and a fantastic blackbox theater.

I take on occasional freelance and consulting work involving theatrical space design, custom hardware, video engineering, and show control. If you have a cool project, I’d love to hear about it.
